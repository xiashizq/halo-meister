# Skull icon sources

These PNG thumbnails were decoded from the installed Halo: Campaign Evolved
IoStore textures under:

`Meteorite/Content/ui/Frontend/CampaignMenu/Textures/skulls`

The cooked `T_Icon_Skull*` assets were converted to legacy texture packages
with `retoc to-legacy`, then their inline DXT1 mip data was decoded to PNG
without changing the artwork. Halo and the artwork are trademarks/copyrights
of Microsoft and their respective owners.
